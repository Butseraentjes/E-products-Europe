Buying for an organisation?

Councils, schools, associations, campsites, restaurants and businesses order several items at a time. That often calls for paperwork a web shop does not provide. Ask for it here — you will hear from the company in your country.

What you can ask us for

  • A quote in your organisation’s name, with a validity date.
  • A purchase order or an invoice with your VAT number and your internal reference.
  • A delivery day agreed with our planning team.
  • The details for a tender or public contract: dimensions, weights and timber species.

For several items our usual quantity discount applies. For a different price we draw up a quote.
