Delivery

We drive the large pieces ourselves.

In our own vans, to Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany and France. A round sets off once it is worthwhile for your region — so the waiting time depends on the season. Our planning team agrees the delivery day with you, and on the day itself you get a message three stops before we arrive. Small items go with GLS.
